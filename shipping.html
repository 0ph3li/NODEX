    Delivery times start when your parcel leaves the bench. Orders placed before 14:00 (Italian time) leave within two working days; weekends and Italian public holidays don’t count.

    CHAPTER 02

    From our bench to your door

    1. You orderYou get a confirmation email with your order number.
    2. We checkEvery item is tested one last time before it’s packed.
    3. We wrapTissue paper, a sticker sheet and a handwritten note.
    4. It shipsTracked courier, tracking link sent by email.
    5. It’s yoursDelivered to your door or a pickup point.

    CHAPTER 03

    Packaging

    Everything ships in a recycled cardboard box with paper padding: no plastic bubbles. Vintage electronics get an extra layer of protection around screens and hinges.

    Sending a gift? Write the recipient’s name in the order notes and we’ll leave the price off the packing slip.

    CHAPTER 04

    Tracking your parcel

    You’ll receive a tracking link as soon as the courier scans your parcel. If the courier can’t deliver, they’ll leave a notice and try again or leave the parcel at a nearby pickup point.

    No update for 3 working days? Write to us with your order number and we’ll chase it.

    CHAPTER 05

    Outside the EU

    Parcels to the UK, Switzerland and Norway travel with all the paperwork already attached. Import VAT and customs duties are set by the destination country and may be collected by the courier on delivery; they aren’t included in our prices.

    CHAPTER 06

    Damaged or missing parcel

    If your parcel arrives damaged, take a photo of the box before opening it and of anything that’s broken, then write to us within 7 days. We’ll send a replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer.

    !

    Parcels are insured until they reach you: if something goes wrong on the way, it’s on us, not you.

    Still stuck?A real person answers within 24 hours, Monday to Saturday.
