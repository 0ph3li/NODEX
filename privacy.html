    What this website keeps on your device

    To work without an account, the site saves a few things in your browser’s local storage. They never leave your device and we can’t see them.

      CHAPTER 04

      Third-party services

      To show our typefaces and run the page animations, your browser downloads files from:

      • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), operated by Google.
      • cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com), operated by Cloudflare.

      Like any website you visit, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the files. We don’t send them anything else.

      CHAPTER 05

      When you order or write to us

      We use your name, email, delivery address, phone number (if you give it) and order details to process your order, deliver it, handle returns and warranty, and answer your messages. The legal basis is the contract between us and, for invoices, our legal obligations.

      We share delivery details only with the courier and payment details only with the payment provider. We keep order records for as long as tax law requires, and messages for as long as needed to help you.

      CHAPTER 06

      Your rights

      Under the GDPR you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or object to how we use it. Just write to us.

      You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

      CHAPTER 07

      Changes to this page

      If we change how we handle data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Last updated: September 2026.
