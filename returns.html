    CHAPTER 01

    30 days to change your mind

    You can return anything you bought from us within 30 days of delivery, for any reason. Returns are free in Italy and the EU.

    Items need to come back complete, with all their accessories, and in the condition you received them. Original packaging is nice but not required.

    !

    This is on top of your legal rights. EU law gives you 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase; we simply give you more time.

    CHAPTER 02

    How to send something back

    1. Write to usUse the contact page with your order number.
    2. Get a labelWe email you a prepaid return label within one working day.
    3. Pack itAny sturdy box will do. Stick the label on top.
    4. Drop it offAt any pickup point of the courier on the label.
    5. RefundWithin 5 working days of the parcel reaching us.

    CHAPTER 03

    Refunds

    We refund the full price of the returned items on the payment method you used. If you paid in 3 instalments, the remaining instalments are cancelled and anything already paid comes back to you.

    Shipping costs you paid on the original order are refunded too when you return the whole order.

    CHAPTER 04

    Exchanges

    Want another colour or a different model? Tell us when you request the return: we’ll ship the new item as soon as the first one is on its way back, so you don’t wait twice.

    CHAPTER 05

    2-year warranty

    Everything we sell, new or vintage, comes with a 2-year warranty from the day of delivery. If something stops working, we’ll repair it, replace it or refund you.

    • Covered: faults in the device, batteries that won’t hold a charge, screens, buttons, hinges, ports, speakers.
    • Not covered: damage from drops, liquids or opening the device, and normal wear on vintage shells.
    • Keep your order number: it’s your proof of purchase.

    CHAPTER 06

    What can’t be returned

    • Earbuds and in-ear headphones once the hygiene seal is opened.
    • Lucky Bags once opened (sealed ones can be returned like anything else).
    • Items damaged after delivery.

    Faulty items are always covered by the warranty, even in these cases.
